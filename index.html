01 · About

About Me

I'm an IT Support Specialist based in Elk Grove, California, with more than three years of hands-on experience supporting technology across a public school district. Since April 2023, I've served as a technical resource for CJUSD School District, responding to help desk tickets, managing district devices, and traveling between school sites to resolve hardware, software, and connectivity issues.

My day-to-day work centers on installing, configuring, and troubleshooting Windows, macOS, and ChromeOS systems, along with performing component-level repairs on desktops, laptops, Chromebooks, tablets, and iPads. I manage devices and user accounts using tools like Microsoft Intune, JAMF MDM, Google Admin Console, Active Directory, and Microsoft 365 — handling software deployment, patch management, task automation, and inventory tracking for district technology.

Beyond break-fix support, I regularly assist with small-to-mid-sized technology projects and system upgrades, support the district's LAN/WAN environment (Wi-Fi, switches, TCP/IP, DHCP, DNS), and maintain detailed documentation, guides, and asset records. I also conduct group and one-on-one technical training for staff, which has sharpened my ability to explain technical concepts clearly to non-technical users.

I hold an Associate of Science in Computer Network Management – Cisco (American River College, 2025) and an Associate of Science in Computer Information Technology (Asher College, 2023), and I'm certified in CompTIA A+, CCNA, and CompTIA Security+. I'm currently building on that foundation with a growing interest in cybersecurity and IT automation, and I'm looking for opportunities in IT support, desktop/systems support, network support, or entry-level cybersecurity roles.
